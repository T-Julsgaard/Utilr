02 what it does

a sticky note that stays on top. and remembers everything by itself.

>always on top

Notes float above every other program, so what you're writing down never gets buried. Turn it off in ⚙.

>nothing to save

Every keystroke is saved. Quit and notr comes back with the same notes, in the same places.

>new notes go beside

A new note slides in right next to the one you're in, never on top of another note.

>keyboard first

tab jumps between notes. ctrl+arrows resizes, alt+arrows moves: tap to nudge, hold to glide.

>keep one as a tab

The floppy saves a note as a numbered tab. Close it and it stays; its tab opens it again.

>seven themes

ghost, bare mono, paper, dots, gutter, green and amber crt. Font, size, bold and italic are kept per theme.

04 the details

  • shortcuts live in a note

    ⚙ → shortcuts writes them into the note. Change a key by editing the text in its [brackets]; it takes effect a moment after you stop typing. The open key is also on utilr's keys page.

  • where your notes live

    %APPDATA%\utilr\tools\notr\notr-state.json, with a .bak of the previous save. Nothing leaves your computer.

  • coming from the old notr

    The first start of utilr copies the stand-alone notr's notes over, so everything comes along. Uninstall the old app afterwards: it would hold the same key.

  • the demo above

    It's notr's real note UI, running in your browser with a small stand-in for the desktop side. Keyboard shortcuts are off there, so your browser's keys keep working.
